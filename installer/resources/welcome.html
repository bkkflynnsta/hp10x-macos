HP Laser 103/107/108 driver for macOS

An open-source driver for the HP Laser 103, 107 and 108 printers (USB). It lets macOS print to these host-based printers, which have no PostScript or PCL support of their own.

It installs:

  • a printer driver, listed as HP Laser 103/107/108 (hp10x)
  • a small background service that stops macOS from taking over the printer's USB connection with its IPP-over-USB service, which these printers don't support properly

After installing, add the printer in System Settings → Printers & Scanners, and choose HP Laser 103/107/108 (hp10x) under Use.

To remove it later, run sudo /Library/Printers/hp10x/uninstall.sh in Terminal.
